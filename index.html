How I Work

Bridging Perception Gaps

Growing up across cultures taught me early that what feels obvious to one person can be invisible to another. That instinct stayed with me. In my work, I pay close attention to where understanding breaks down so teams can align before momentum is lost.

The Art of Alignment

To me, strategy is not just planning. It is creating the conditions for people to move in the same direction. I care about both structure and emotion because a system only works when it makes sense and resonates with the people inside it.

Navigating the Unpaved Path

I do my best work before the path is fully defined. I am most energized in early-stage, ambiguous environments where the challenge is not just improving what exists, but shaping what should exist in the first place.

About Me

My work sits between structure and imagination. I have spent more than a decade building cross-border partnerships and strategic programs in film and media, and more recently expanding that practice into research, business innovation, and experience strategy through graduate work in the U.S. Across those settings, I have been most drawn to the same challenge: how to turn complexity into something people can align around and move with.
