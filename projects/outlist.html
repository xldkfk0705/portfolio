Maker SCAD 2026

Building a Decision Engine with AI-Assisted Prototyping

"We didn't need more recommendations. We needed a better way to re-find, decide, and go."

The Challenge

Planning something simple with friends often took too much effort. Links disappeared into chat, screenshots got buried in the photo gallery, and saved places were scattered across apps. The problem was not a lack of options. It was the lack of structure needed to re-find, compare, and decide.

The Insight

Re-finding was the real bottleneck. As retrieving options later felt too difficult, people often did not even try. Participants responded strongly to the idea of having one place to save, but if saving felt too manual, they would stop. And if comparing choices during coordination was messy, no one would commit. What I needed to build was lightweight decision infrastructure.

The Approach

I built a functional pilot using Lovable and natural language prompting, treating AI as a collaborative build partner for shaping database logic, flows, and feature pathways. The product centered on a shared saving layer with AI-assisted extraction, structured comparison, and group decision-making.

The Outcome

User testing suggested that structured saving reduced perceived coordination friction and made the planning process feel more manageable. The group coordination layer still needs further testing.
