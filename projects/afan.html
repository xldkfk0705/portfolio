AFAN

Program Architect Korean Film Council (KOFIC) 2023

Governance as a Creative Act

"The problem was never diplomacy. It was governance."

The Challenge

Relaunch a dormant multilateral initiative under post-pandemic constraints, including leadership transition, tighter budgets, and no consensus on formal organizational structure. The task was to navigate diplomatic complexity across diverse member states while building support for a model that could make meaningful collaboration possible.

The Insight

The problem was not diplomacy. It was the structure itself. After reviewing prior records and proposals, I identified that the model was too binding to move. What the initiative needed was not more endorsement, but a lighter framework that made participation easier.

The Approach

I redesigned the governance model by removing the formal membership structure, replacing it with a simple opt-in agreement, and shifting the initiative from a centralized institution to a flexible network. I then briefed leadership to position the Network Model with partner agencies.

The Outcome

AFAN moved from symbolic diplomacy to a working collaboration platform. It launched with seven founding agencies, later expanded to eight, and now supports recurring programs while connecting Asian film agencies with European partners through EFAD.
