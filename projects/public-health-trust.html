Rebuilding Public Health Trust

Strategy Lead SCADpro x Deloitte 2025

Designing a Communication Framework for Post-Pandemic Recovery

"Trust had eroded. The challenge was not only what to say, but how to rebuild credibility before the next crisis arrived."

The Challenge

A U.S. federal public health agency needed a communication strategy that could help restore trust after pandemic-era miscommunication had weakened confidence in its messaging. The system had to serve two very different audiences: healthcare professionals seeking detailed, evidence-based information, and the general public needing guidance that was clear, accessible, and actionable. It also had to remain usable in future emergency situations, where speed and clarity are both critical.

The Insight

The issue was not just message content. It was trust infrastructure. Through landscape analysis, expert interviews, and benchmarking, we found that public health messaging becomes more credible when it is delivered through trusted peers and reinforced at the community level, rather than communicated only from a distant institutional center.

The Approach

I helped lead strategy development across research, concept framing, and final delivery. Our team built a trust-centered communication framework grounded in expert insight and competitive benchmarking, then translated it into practical tools such as workflow recommendations, channel-specific templates, and scenario-based examples designed for real-world use.

The Outcome

We delivered a communication framework that addressed the needs of both professional and public audiences while creating a more adaptable foundation for future crisis response. The final system paired strategic direction with practical tools, making it easier for stakeholders to apply the work in both routine and high-pressure communication contexts.
