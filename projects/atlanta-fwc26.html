Creative Strategist SCAD 2026

Designing the Epicenter System

"The Last Mile was not just a route. It was a chance to turn movement into momentum."

The Challenge

Design a cohesive, human-centered Last Mile experience that brings together brand narrative, cultural expression, emotional storytelling, and clear, resilient wayfinding, so fans can move confidently while experiencing Atlanta's identity at every touchpoint.

The Insight

Atlanta was already positioned as the epicenter of soccer. Building on that idea, we synthesized research on the city's cultural history and aligned it with FIFA Atlanta's identity system. That led us to reframe the Last Mile as more than a route. It became an opportunity to express Atlanta's character while building anticipation on the way to the stadium.

The Approach

We translated the idea of the epicenter into a progression of energy moving toward the stadium, using zone logic, heatmap color progression, increasing arrow density, and messaging that builds in intensity. As proximity increased, the environment became warmer, more compressed, and more charged, shifting from welcome to collective momentum.

The Outcome

The project has expanded beyond the Last Mile into a broader citywide progression. We developed an operational playbook that gives partners and vendors a shared framework for executing the experience consistently across the city.
