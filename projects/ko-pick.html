KO-PICK Showcase

Program Lead Korean Film Council (KOFIC) 2022–2024

From Spectacle to Matchmaking

"Visibility wasn't the bottleneck. Accessibility was."

The Challenge

After the global rise of Korean content, awareness was no longer the issue. But KOFIC's international promotion model was still centered on receptions and brand events that generated visibility without creating enough pathways to transaction. International partners were interested, but they lacked a clear way to identify market-ready Korean projects or connect with the right co-production counterparts.

The Insight

The gap was not demand. It was access. What international partners needed was not another promotional event, but a more structured way to discover viable projects and meet the right people around them.

The Approach

I redirected resources from standalone promotional events into KO-PICK Showcase, a curated B2B matchmaking platform embedded within major film markets. The program was designed to make Korean film IP more legible, accessible, and actionable for international buyers and co-production partners.

The Outcome

In its first year, the program facilitated more than 225 B2B meetings. By year two, it expanded from four to seven global markets. KOFIC's role shifted from host to facilitator, creating repeatable infrastructure for Korean IP licensing and co-production partnerships.
